# FastZen 1.2 public copy

The French and English catalogue descriptions now introduce the free seven-day
preview and the date-specific changes available with Custom Week (Semaine sur
mesure). The App Store public lookup returned version 1.2.0 for France and the
United States on 9 September 2026 at 21:36 UTC, with a public release timestamp
of 21:16:31 UTC. Custom Week remains a one-time purchase; these descriptions
do not add a health or outcome claim.

The original public JSON responses and their SHA-256 hashes are retained with
the FastZen release evidence, in `site-pending/publication-1.2.0/public-storefronts/`.
The French record in `public-store-readback.json` has its own new timestamp;
the other entries retain their previous dates.

The current catalogue displays the FastZen icon and text. Its unused historical
preview assets are not rendered by the current generator and were not replaced.
The app website has its own eight-language release and native capture review.

Validation: generated HTML and catalogue checks, the local site guard and its
required checks, then French/English browser review at mobile, tablet and desktop
sizes. Publication and served-content verification are recorded separately in
the release artifacts; this document alone is not deployment evidence.
