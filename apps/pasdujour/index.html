Pas du Jour

Apple Watch

App Store

Six styles are free. A guide in the app explains how to add the complication. The Pro editor and custom profiles are a one-time purchase. Access from before version 1.1.0 remains.

Steps are read from Health on the device, with your permission. How often the face refreshes depends on the watch and those permissions.

Pas du Jour is not a medical device. It does not diagnose and it does not give medical advice. There is no account and no server.
