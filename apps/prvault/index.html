Record tracking is free. You can keep favorites, read history, log a result with its date and units, and preview a CSV before import.

Pro adds Today's Session. It turns a percentage plan into working weights from your own one-rep max, shows the plates to load, and lets you tick off sets. Opening a target in ColdLoad does not create a PRVault record.

Data stays on the device, with optional iCloud sync. There is no PRVault account.
