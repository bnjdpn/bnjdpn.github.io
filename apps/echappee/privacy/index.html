Échappée. Privacy policy

Privacy policy — iPhone, iPad, Mac

Android

Échappée Android (com.bnjdpn.echappee), published by Benjamin Dupin.

Échappée needs no Échappée account, game server or ads, and does not run its own analytics. Settings, workouts, activities and sensor measurements stay on your device. These data prepare and record your rides and display your sports measurements. They stay stored locally until deleted or the app is uninstalled. No remote access to these data is provided.

Bluetooth scanning finds your sensors. Échappée does not record your location. Android 11 and earlier require location permission for this scan.

Google Play handles the one-time purchase and restoration under its own terms. Échappée receives a signed purchase receipt. The receipt and the two-ride trial counter are encrypted locally with an Android Keystore key. Android backups and game exports exclude them. Uninstalling removes local data and the trial counter. Your purchase remains associated with your Google Play account.

The Google Play Billing library included in the Android app may send diagnostics to Google when handling purchases or restoration. These diagnostics are collected and shared with Google for analytics. Purchase history is collected to provide the purchase and restoration features. Échappée does not send your workouts, activities or sensor measurements to Google for these purposes.

You can export activities and send the files to another service yourself. Échappée does not transmit them automatically.

For questions or requests about your data, use the support form, choosing “Privacy”. If you use this form, Cloudflare and Grok Bot process your reply address and message to handle your request. Messages are kept while handling your request, then deleted. You can request access, correction or deletion through the same form. The game does not send your activities to this service.
