Baby and family

Petites Dents

iPhone

The free record covers the 20 primary teeth, teething and eruption dates, age to the day, notes, history, more than one child profile, a PDF dental chart, and the first photo of each child. There is no account.

One purchase, with Family Sharing, adds the second set of teeth, more photos, and keepsake PDFs and cards. Previewing a keepsake stays free. The dental chart PDF for a clinician does not include a photo.

Petites Dents is a personal log, not a medical device. Photos stay on the device until you export them.
