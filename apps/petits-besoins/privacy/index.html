Petits Besoins. Privacy policy

Petits Besoins is in preparation for iOS and Android. It is not yet available in either store.

Policy date: 2026-10-22

Scope

Benjamin Dupin publishes Petits Besoins for iOS and Android, identified by com.bnjdpn.petitsbesoins. This policy covers the versions in preparation.

Child information

An adult may enter a child name or pseudonym, potty and diaper entries, dates, times, home or daycare setting, notes and kit instructions. These data stay on the device. Benjamin Dupin does not receive them.

Local storage

The iOS version stores the notebook with SwiftData and local preferences. It has no account, server or online sync. Android uses Room and DataStore. Android backup and device transfer are disabled. Petits Besoins provides no backup service. On iOS, the notebook directory is excluded from device backup. Operating system backups may include preferences according to device settings. Do not rely on them to recover the notebook.

Network and collection

Petits Besoins has no ads, analytics or third-party crash reporting. It does not transmit notebook entries to Benjamin Dupin or a server. On iOS, StoreKit communicates with Apple for product information, purchases and restoration. On Android, the Google Play Billing library communicates with Google Play for the product, the purchase and its restoration. It adds the Internet permission for this purpose only.

Local reminders

The operating system schedules and displays local notifications. Permission is requested when reminders are enabled. On Android, the startup permission lets the app reschedule reminders after a restart. Reminder scheduling does not send notebook data.

PDF and text sharing

PDFs are generated on the device and contain kit instructions and blank sheets, not the recorded history. The child name is hidden by default. Including it requires an explicit selection. Daily text summaries can also be shared. The chosen receiving app handles the shared copy under its own policy.

Purchases

Apple processes the iOS one-time purchase through StoreKit. The app checks entitlement to PDF export and reusable sheets. It does not collect payment card information. Google Play processes the Android one-time purchase through Google Play Billing. The app reads the purchase state to unlock the same features. Debug builds keep a local simulation without payment.

Retention and deletion

Entries remain on the device until they are deleted or app data are removed. Uninstalling removes local data. Android also permits clearing app storage in device settings. PDFs and text shared elsewhere remain until those copies are deleted.

Contact

For a question about the app or its data, use the protected contact form or email petites@bnjdpn.com. Avoid including personal information about a child.

The form is protected against spam by Cloudflare Turnstile, which analyzes technical browser signals (including the IP address) to distinguish a person from a bot. Turnstile is loaded only on the page containing the contact form, not on support or privacy pages. The approximate country (derived from the IP address) and referring site accompany the message. The message is then forwarded by Cloudflare (Workers and Email Routing) to the developer's support inbox. Messages received are read and handled by Grok Bot, an automated assistant (AI) used by the publisher to sort them and prepare or send replies; the publisher remains responsible for this processing. These data are used to answer your request and fix the app, based on our legitimate interest, or performance of the contract if your request concerns a purchase. They are not used for marketing. Closed requests with no exchange for more than 12 months are manually deleted during a quarterly review, no later than 15 months after the last exchange. To request access, correction, erasure or restriction, or to object to processing, email petites@bnjdpn.com. You may also lodge a complaint with the CNIL.
