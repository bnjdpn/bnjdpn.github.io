Petits Besoins. Support

Petits Besoins for Android is available on Google Play through a staged rollout. The iOS version is under review.

Set reminders

Open Settings on iOS or Reminders on Android. Enable reminders, then choose the interval and daily window. Notification permission is requested when reminders are enabled. Snooze postpones a reminder by 15 minutes. Device settings may delay notification delivery.

Preview and export the kit

The kit contains the family instructions and blank weekly and tracking sheets. It does not export the recorded history. Its complete preview is free. The child name is hidden by default. The share sheet opens only after an export is requested.

Purchases and restoration

A one-time purchase through StoreKit on iOS or Google Play Billing on Android unlocks PDF export and reusable sheets. The offer displays the price provided by the store. Use Restore purchases in Settings to retrieve an existing entitlement. Closing the offer keeps the draft.

Change the language

The app follows the device language. French and English are included. iOS also includes British English. There is no language selector in the app.

Delete data

Individual entries can be deleted from History. Uninstalling the app removes its local data. On Android, clearing app storage also removes them. Copies shared elsewhere must be deleted in the receiving app or storage location.

Contact

For a question about the app or its data, use the protected contact form or email petites@bnjdpn.com. Avoid including personal information about a child.

The form is protected against spam by Cloudflare Turnstile, which analyzes technical browser signals (including the IP address) to distinguish a person from a bot. Turnstile is loaded only on the page containing the contact form, not on support or privacy pages. The approximate country (derived from the IP address) and referring site accompany the message. The message is then forwarded by Cloudflare (Workers and Email Routing) to the developer's support inbox. Messages received are read and handled by Grok Bot, an automated assistant (AI) used by the publisher to sort them and prepare or send replies; the publisher remains responsible for this processing. These data are used to answer your request and fix the app, based on our legitimate interest, or performance of the contract if your request concerns a purchase. They are not used for marketing. Closed requests with no exchange for more than 12 months are manually deleted during a quarterly review, no later than 15 months after the last exchange. To request access, correction, erasure or restriction, or to object to processing, email petites@bnjdpn.com. You may also lodge a complaint with the CNIL.
