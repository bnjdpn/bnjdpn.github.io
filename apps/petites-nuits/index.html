Baby and family

Petites Nuits

iPhone

contact@bnjdpn.com

You choose Night or Nap, enter the times, and can note wakeups, feeds, mood and other observations. The diary shows recorded time and does not count an overlap twice. It only knows the entries you write.

Recording, the calendar, the chart and a full PDF preview are free. Sharing, printing and saving the PDF is a one-time purchase. The file is made on the iPhone for 7, 14 or 28 days.

The app describes what you recorded. It does not predict the next nap, it does not run a sleep program, and it does not give medical advice.
