MoveAtlas

iPhone

contact@bnjdpn.com

Names can be searched in English, French, German and Spanish. Filters cover body area, muscle and equipment. The active gym limits results to its equipment.

A machine card can store a setting, a note and a local photo. An exercise without a machine can store a note. Pages show text instructions and no third-party exercise animations.

The atlas, search, favorites, saved workouts and gym profiles are free. The notebook includes 5 items, or 25 for eligible users from before version 1.1.0. One purchase removes that limit. Notes and photos stay on the device.
