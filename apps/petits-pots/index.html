Petits Pots

Petits Pots is available on Android. The iOS app is awaiting App Store review.

Get it on Google Play

Track each batch

Record bags of frozen cubes, jars and pouches with their quantities, dates and available amounts. Each bag keeps its own cube weight and storage date. Expired batches stay visible and are never deleted automatically.

Shop from your stock

See the foods you have recorded as running low, finished or needing a refill, grouped in one shopping list. Track starches, herbs and fats too. Checking off a purchase does not add food to your stock: you record the quantities yourself.

Plan with what you have

Adjust your lunch, snack and dinner templates, then generate one day of meals from your supplies. Review or regenerate the preview before confirming it. Confirmation reserves the quantities; marking a meal as served updates your journal without subtracting them twice.

Free features and Pro

Stock, shopping lists, the journal, editable active templates and one day of meal planning are free. Petits Pots Pro unlocks planning for 2 to 21 days and a library of multiple personal template sets through an optional one-time purchase, with no subscription. The price is shown in the app before you buy.

Your data on your phone

Your stock and menus are stored locally. No Petits Pots account and no ads.

Storage dates and medical advice

Calculated freezer storage dates are indicative and can be changed. For jars and pouches, enter the date on the packaging. Petits Pots cannot check the cold chain and does not replace medical advice or guidance from your healthcare professional.

Contact

For a question about the app or its data, use the protected contact form or email petites@bnjdpn.com. Avoid including personal information about a child.

The form is protected against spam by Cloudflare Turnstile, which analyzes technical browser signals (including the IP address) to distinguish a person from a bot. Turnstile is loaded only on the page containing the contact form, not on support or privacy pages. The approximate country (derived from the IP address) and referring site accompany the message. The message is then forwarded by Cloudflare (Workers and Email Routing) to the developer's support inbox. Messages received are read and handled by Grok Bot, an automated assistant (AI) used by the publisher to sort them and prepare or send replies; the publisher remains responsible for this processing. These data are used to answer your request and fix the app, based on our legitimate interest, or performance of the contract if your request concerns a purchase. They are not used for marketing. Closed requests with no exchange for more than 12 months are manually deleted during a quarterly review, no later than 15 months after the last exchange. To request access, correction, erasure or restriction, or to object to processing, email petites@bnjdpn.com. You may also lodge a complaint with the CNIL.
