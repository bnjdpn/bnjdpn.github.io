Catminers

Available on iPhone, iPad and Android.

Explore a gallery

Open safe cells, flag mines and use the numbers to make your next move. The first cell is safe. The campaign crosses ten worlds, with daily challenges and custom boards alongside it.

Welcome companions

Wins earn fish and unlock cats. Each companion brings a power: a safe hint, a marked mine, protection or extra fish. Your refuge shows the next cat to welcome.

Free progression, optional purchases

Every level is playable without a purchase. The shop offers fish packs and a one-time pass. The pass adds 50% to fish earned with every win and grants 5,000 fish on the initial purchase. Prices are those displayed by Apple or Google. There are no subscriptions or ads.
