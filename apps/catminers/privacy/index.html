Catminers. Privacy policy

A feline minesweeper game with a campaign, daily challenges and an animated refuge.

Policy date: 2026-10-24

Application

This policy covers Catminers, identifier com.bnjdpn.catminers, published by Benjamin Dupin.

Game data

The game stores progress, wins, fish, lives and recharge time, owned cats and coats, and preferences on your device. It needs no Catminers account and does not send this data to the developer. It contains no advertising or analytics tools.

Purchases

Apple or Google processes payments using your store account. The developer does not receive your card details. To prevent duplicate credits, the game keeps Apple transaction identifiers or Google token digests locally. They are not sent to a developer server. The store retains transaction data under its own policy.

On Android, the Google Play Billing library also sends Google technical diagnostics about purchase operations: game and library versions, device model and system, memory and network information. This can happen when connecting to the billing service, even without a purchase. Google processes this information under its privacy policy. These diagnostics go to Google, rather than to a developer server. Your progress and saved game content stay on your device.

Backups, removal and contact

The app has no sync service of its own. Apple or Google system backups may include game data, depending on your settings. Removing app data removes its local copy; records held by the store follow that store’s rules.

Support messages are used to respond to your request. The contact form goes through Cloudflare (Turnstile against spam, Workers for delivery) and attaches no game data. For data questions, use the same form.
