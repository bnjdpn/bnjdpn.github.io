Petites Gouttes. Privacy policy

The privacy policy named on the App Store listing is published at the link below.

https://bnjdpn.com/petites-gouttes/en-US/privacy

For questions about that policy, use Contact.

The form is protected against spam by Cloudflare Turnstile, which analyzes technical browser signals (including the IP address) to distinguish a person from a bot. Turnstile is loaded only on the app page that contains the contact form, not on these pages. The message is then forwarded by Cloudflare (Workers and Email Routing) to the developer's support inbox.
