BrewMeter for Android: privacy notice

Last updated: 23 October 2026.

BrewMeter for Android is a caffeine journal and estimation app. You do not need an app account. The app has no advertising or publisher-operated behavioral analytics. Google Play Billing sends the technical diagnostics described below.

Data kept on your device

Drinks, amounts, times, favorites, custom beverages, settings, estimates and reminder preferences are stored on your device in the app's Room database and DataStore. A verified Google Play purchase receipt is cached on your device to restore Pro access. The publisher does not receive your journal or sleep data through an app server.

Optional Health Connect access

If you enable caffeine writing and grant Health Connect permission, BrewMeter writes the caffeine amount of drinks you log as Nutrition records. When you correct or delete a journal entry, the app attempts to reconcile the record it created. It does not read nutrition records from other apps. If you separately grant sleep permission, BrewMeter reads recent sleep sessions to show local comparisons with your recorded caffeine and an optional bedtime suggestion. These comparisons do not diagnose a condition or predict sleep. You can turn off caffeine writing in the app and revoke either Health Connect permission in Android settings. Records already written to Health Connect are managed there; uninstalling BrewMeter should not be treated as deletion of those records.

Optional notification and export

If you turn on the bedtime reminder, the app asks for notification permission and schedules a local notification. You can turn it off or revoke permission in Android settings. If you choose CSV export, Android asks you where to save the file. BrewMeter writes the selected journal data to that location; further handling depends on the destination you choose.

Purchases and support

Google Play processes an optional one-time Pro purchase. BrewMeter uses the purchase result to verify and restore your entitlement. When the app connects or resumes, it can automatically query Google Play for existing purchases, update Pro access and acknowledge verified purchases by sending their purchase token to Google Play. These checks can occur without a new purchase action. The app does not receive payment-card details. Google's terms and privacy policy govern its payment service.

When the app connects to Google Play Billing, its library sends operational diagnostics directly to Google over an encrypted connection. This can happen when the app opens or resumes, even if you do not buy Pro. These diagnostics can include the Billing operation and response, diagnostic messages, callback timing, a randomly generated Billing client/session identifier, Android version and build details, device model and manufacturer, app version, device language and country, time zone, SIM/network type and event timestamps. These diagnostics do not include your caffeine journal or sleep records. Google handles these diagnostics under its own privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy.

The separate BrewMeter support website has an optional contact form. If you choose to use it, the details you enter are processed through Cloudflare Workers and forwarded to the developer's support inbox. Grok Bot, the publisher's automated assistant, may sort messages and prepare or send replies. The website's privacy notice explains that separate flow. Opening the app does not send your journal to the support form.

Your choices

You can edit or delete individual entries in the app, revoke Health Connect and notification permissions, and remove the app's local data through Android settings or by uninstalling. Health Connect records and exported CSV files may remain outside the app; manage or delete them in their respective destinations. Deleting local app data does not delete diagnostics or purchase history held by Google. BrewMeter has no in-app mechanism to request deletion of those Google-held records, and a support-form request does not delete them. For privacy or support requests, use the contact form at https://bnjdpn.com/BrewMeter/#contact.
