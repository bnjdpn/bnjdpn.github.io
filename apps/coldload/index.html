ColdLoad

iPhone, iPad

contact@bnjdpn.com

Enter a target. If the plates on hand cannot hit it, the nearest lower load and the nearest higher load stay separate choices. A reverse mode totals a bar that is already loaded.

Bar profiles include 20 kg, 15 kg and a custom bar. The calculation can use every plate, or only the pairs saved in a local stock.

The calculation stays on the device. There is no account. Pro is a one-time purchase for more saved gyms and for percentages of a reference load.
