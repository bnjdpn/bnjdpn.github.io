after I joined.

The companies provide context. The delivery remains the point.

A monolith made progressively changeable.

  • Decomposed responsibilities into autonomous services.
  • Secured front-end and back-end contracts with OpenAPI and Pact tests.
  • Migrated the delivery pipeline from Travis CI to GitHub Actions.

2023—2025 · Fnac Darty

Darty’s catalogue moved to Elasticsearch 8.

  • Migrated the search platform from version 6 to version 8.
  • Designed, developed and deployed a new Azure application.
  • Delivered application migrations without reducing business service.

2020—2023 · SGCIB

A platform and its team brought forward together.

  • Moved Java 7 to Java 17 and Spring Boot 2.3 to 3.1.
  • Led the technical growth of a three-developer feature team.
  • Treated architecture, technical debt, hiring and knowledge transfer as one system.
