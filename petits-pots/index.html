Everyday questions

How do storage dates work?

For purchased products, enter the date on the packaging. Dates calculated from a storage duration are indicative. Petits Pots cannot check the cold chain and does not replace guidance from your healthcare professional.

Where can I see the week?

The weekly preview brings the days and meals together. Planning multiple days and exporting an image or PDF are part of Pro.
